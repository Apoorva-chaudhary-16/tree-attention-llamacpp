# Travel Guide: Lisbon

Lisbon is the capital of Portugal and sits on seven hills along the Tagus river.

## Getting there

### By air
Humberto Delgado Airport is 7 km from the centre. The metro red line reaches the city in 20 minutes and a ticket costs 1.85 euros.

### By train
Trains from Porto take about 3 hours and leave every hour.

## Where to stay

- **Alfama**: old streets, small guesthouses, no car access
- **Baixa**: flat and central, good for first visits
- **Bairro Alto**: nightlife, noisy after midnight

## Food

The famous pastry is the pastel de nata. The best-known bakery sells one for 1.40 euros and opens at 8 am. For lunch try bacalhau, a salted cod dish.

## Day trips

### Sintra
Sintra is 40 minutes away by train. Visit the Pena Palace early to avoid queues.

### Cascais
Cascais is a beach town 35 minutes from Lisbon by train.

## Practical tips

> Wear comfortable shoes. The cobblestones are slippery when wet.

Trams 28 and 15 are popular with tourists and often crowded.
